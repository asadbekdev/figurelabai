# Open questions and contradictions

## Verified contradictions in current official surfaces

1. **Signup credits**
   - Public hero/product pages say `Get 200 free credits`.
   - Pricing/help docs say `150 one-time credits` for new users.
   - The inspected signed-in account displayed `200` credits, but its grant source is unknown.

2. **Referral reward amount and unit**
   - Signed referral page says `Give 300, Get 300` credits.
   - Linked official rules say 500 **points** for inviter and invitee.
   - The rules page also contains a placeholder date: `October [day], 2025`.

3. **Referral reward lifetime**
   - Rules say invitee points are valid for 30 days.
   - Referral hero does not disclose expiry.

4. **Pro daily refresh**
   - Pricing/help prose says paid plans get 100 daily credits.
   - Help-center comparison table shows `0` for Pro.

5. **Top-up pack**
   - Current pricing list says 1,000 credits for $15.
   - Another FAQ example says 1,500 credits for $15.

6. **Annual savings**
   - Pricing UI says Save 45%, while cards show 44%/45% and help copy says save 50%.

7. **Plot export**
   - Public copy promises PPT.
   - Published editor screenshot export menu shows PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF, and Download Code, but no PPT.

8. **Support email domain**
   - Most pages use `support@figurelabs.ai`.
   - One public footer link uses `support@figurelabs.com`.
